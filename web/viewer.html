    Arena rules
    Same seed, same game: this viewer runs the identical simulation the headless runner uses (compiled to WebAssembly). Keys: space play/pause, ← → step back/forward one second, F follow/unfollow. Link a game with #seed-N.

    Evolution

    Each strategy is a genome of 13 drive weights. A population of 42 genomes plays together in seeded games; the best half survive and keep being re-tested, the rest of the next generation are their mutated children. Nobody is told what to do. Pick a run:

    What it found

    Lineage

    Height = share of the population. Colour = founder lineage; each band is a kin clan (a heritable tag: one birth in ten starts a new clan, nested inside the one it split from), so you can watch dynasties rise and get displaced. Hover a band for what makes that clan different; click it to trace its best genome back to its founder.

    Which traits rise

    Population mean (line) and the middle 80% (band) of each gene, generation by generation, on the gene's full range. Outlined green if it ended well above where it started, red if well below.

    Behaviour and politics

    What the population actually did, per game: the named political moments, how much of the time agents spent shadowing or sheltering, and whether kin really spare kin (kin share of all cell-eating vs. what blind eating would give).

    Signature moments

    Replays from the run itself. Watch loads that generation's 42 genomes into the arena on the same seed, so the game replays exactly, and follows the protagonist (white ring); the other party gets an orange dashed ring.
